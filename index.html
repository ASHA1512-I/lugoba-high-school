Welcome to our school

A Centre of Learning, Character & Discipline

Lugoba High School is a co-educational secondary school dedicated to developing well-rounded students. Guided by our motto, “The Sky Is The Limit”, we inspire every learner to reach beyond their perceived limits.

With experienced teachers, modern facilities and a caring environment, we combine rigorous academics with sports, arts and leadership to prepare students for life and further studies.

  • Qualified, dedicated teaching staff
  • Well-equipped science & computer laboratories
  • Strong focus on discipline and good values
  • Vibrant sports and co-curricular programmes
