How to Apply

Admissions for Form One and Form Five are now open. Follow these simple steps to join the Lugoba family.

01

Enquire

Contact our admissions office or fill the enquiry form below to express interest.

02

Collect & Submit Form

Obtain an application form, complete it and submit with the required documents.

03

Interview / Assessment

Shortlisted applicants attend a short entrance assessment and interview.

04

Admission & Enrolment

Successful applicants receive an offer, pay fees and report for registration.

Requirements

What You Need

📝

Form One Entry

  • Completed Standard Seven (PSLE)
  • Primary school leaving certificate / results
  • Birth certificate copy
  • Four passport-size photographs
  • Completed application form
🎓

Form Five Entry

  • Form Four (CSEE) certificate / results
  • Meets the required division & points
  • Birth certificate copy
  • Four passport-size photographs
  • Completed application form
