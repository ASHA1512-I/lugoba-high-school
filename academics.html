Our Programmes

Levels We Offer

From Form One through Form Six, we deliver a balanced curriculum that prepares students for national examinations and beyond.

Form 1 – 4

Ordinary Level (O-Level)

A broad foundation covering languages, mathematics, sciences, humanities and ICT, leading to the Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (CSEE).

Form 5 – 6

Advanced Level (A-Level)

Specialised Science and Arts combinations preparing students for the Advanced Certificate (ACSEE) and university entry.

Subjects

Streams & Combinations

🔭

Science Stream

Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics. Combinations include PCB, PCM and CBG for aspiring doctors, engineers and scientists.

📜

Arts Stream

History, Geography, Economics, Kiswahili, English and more — with combinations such as HGE and HGL for future leaders and professionals.

💻

ICT & Skills

Computer studies and practical skills integrated across the curriculum to prepare students for a digital world.
