Arts & Sciences

Lugoba High School offers secondary education in both Arts and Sciences, supporting learners at O-Level and A-Level.

Our day and boarding school environment gives students space to learn, grow and prepare for the next stage of their education.

For full subject choices, combinations and entry requirements, please contact the school office directly.
