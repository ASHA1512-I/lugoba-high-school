Who we are

Lugoba High School

Lugoba High School offers day and boarding secondary education in both Arts and Sciences for O-Level and A-Level students.

Guided by our motto, “The Sky Is The Limit”, we work to give young people quality learning opportunities for the future.

Our school is located on Lugoba–Katooke Road, P.O. Box 22877, Kampala.
