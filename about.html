About Us

Welcome to Lugoba High School

Learning, character and community.

Who We Are

Growing Learners for Tomorrow

Lugoba High School is a secondary school in Nabweru, Wakiso District, Uganda. We offer secondary education from Senior One to Senior Six, with a focus on learning, discipline and character.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, responsible learners who are prepared for further study and life.

Our Mission

To provide a supportive learning environment that develops knowledge, skills and good character.

Our Focus

Learning, Character & Community

We support learners to grow academically and personally throughout their secondary education.

Our School Community

Working together

Students, families and staff contribute to a supportive learning environment.

Lugoba High School
