Who We Are

Our Story

Lugoba High School was founded to bring quality, affordable secondary education to the community and beyond. Over the years it has grown into a respected institution known for discipline, academic performance and holistic development.

Our name and badge carry a proud identity. The torch stands for knowledge and enlightenment, the open book and pen for learning and wisdom, while our motto “The Sky Is The Limit” reminds every student that there is no ceiling to what they can achieve.

Today we serve both boarding and day students across O-Level and A-Level, offering a safe, nurturing environment where young people flourish.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide quality, learner-centred education that develops knowledgeable, skilled, disciplined and responsible citizens who can contribute positively to society and compete globally.

👁

Our Vision

To be a leading secondary school recognised for academic excellence, strong moral values and the all-round development of every learner — because the sky is the limit.

Our Journey

Milestones Over the Years

2001

School Founded

Lugoba High School opens its doors with its first intake of O-Level students.

2008

A-Level Introduced

Science and Arts combinations added for Form 5 and Form 6 students.

2014

New Laboratories & Library

Modern science labs, a computer lab and a well-stocked library are completed.

2020

Boarding Expansion

New hostels built to welcome more boarding students from across the region.

2026

Record Results

The school celebrates a 98% national examination pass rate.
