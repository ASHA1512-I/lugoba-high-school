Frequently asked questions

Quick Answers

Which academic levels does Lugoba offer?

Lugoba High School offers O-Level and A-Level education.


Which programmes are available?

The school offers Arts and Sciences. Contact the office for current subject choices and combinations.


Can my child join as a day scholar or boarder?

The school offers both day and boarding options. The office can confirm current arrangements.

How do I ask about admission?

Call the school office for current vacancies, requirements, fees and application guidance.


Where is the school?

Lugoba–Katooke Road, P.O. Box 22877, Kampala.


How can I get current event dates?

Please contact the office directly for confirmed notices, reporting dates and school events.
